Your payment has three parts

Most people shop by price. Price is the number that gets you in the door. The payment is the number you live with, and it's the one the lender actually approves. Here's what it looks like on a median-priced Lawton home right now. On an FHA or conventional loan there's a fourth line, monthly mortgage insurance, and it runs around $90 to $100 on a house this size.

The one that catches people

Insurance is about 18% of that payment. In a lot of states it's half that. Oklahoma carries some of the highest homeowners premiums in the country because of hail and wind, and it's been climbing around 5% a year. Get a quote on a house before you're under contract, not after. I'll tell you when to make that call.

The process

How this actually goes

1

We talk about the payment first

Before we look at a single house. You tell me what you're comfortable paying every month, and I'll tell you what price that buys in this market. It saves everybody a lot of time.

2

You get pre-approved

Your lender, whoever you pick. A pre-approval is what makes your offer real to a seller. Without one you're just browsing, and in this market that costs you houses. If you don't have a lender yet I'm happy to give you a few names, and you should talk to more than one. Rates and fees vary and it's worth shopping.

3

We go look

I'll send you what's new and what's been sitting. If you can't get out to a place, I'll walk it on video and send it to you. That happens a lot with folks still at their last duty station.

4

We write the offer

Homes here are closing at about 98.5% of asking, so there usually isn't much room to cut the price. There's often real room in seller-paid closing costs and points. Those move your payment further than a price cut does, and sellers say yes to them more often.

5

Inspection, appraisal, insurance

This is where deadlines matter and where deals go sideways if nobody's watching. I track every date and tell you what's coming before it's due.

6

Closing

Around 30 to 45 days from contract on most loans. I'll be there.

What that means for you

You've probably read that it's a buyer's market. Nationally that's true, and it's the strongest one on record. Lawton isn't running that way. Supply here is tighter than the country's, half of everything sells inside four weeks, and sellers are still getting about 98.5% of what they ask. Houses that have been sitting a while are where the room to negotiate is, and finding those is my job.
